Detector: STA/LTA on IU.MAJO (1-8 Hz band, STA 1 s / LTA 30 s, threshold 10). Tested on 10 earthquakes (M5.8-6.0, Japan, 2011-2023): detected 10/10, median delay 48.5 s after origin time. In 24 random quiet hours the single station raised 18 alarms (0.75/h). Requiring confirmation by at least 2 of 4 neighboring stations (JP.JGF, JP.JSD, G.INU, PS.TSK, within ±60 s) leaves 5 alarms (0.21/h) and still confirms 9/10 earthquakes (the 10th, Tohoku 2011, could not be checked: only one neighbor had a full record). Limitations: only M5.8-6.0 events tested; remaining alarms were not checked against a local catalogue and may be small real events; thresholds were tuned on the same data.
