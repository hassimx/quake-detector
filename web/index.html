    How it works

    1. Each station: STA/LTA

    Every station's signal is filtered to 1-8 Hz, where earthquake waves are strong. The detector compares the average signal level over the last 1 second (STA, short term) with the average over the last 30 seconds (LTA, long term). Quiet noise gives a ratio near 1; when the ratio exceeds 10, the station raises an alarm.

    2. Network: at least 3 stations within 60 s

    A single station can be fooled by a local disturbance (a truck, wind, a sensor glitch). An earthquake is seen by several stations at once. So alarms from different stations less than 60 s apart are grouped into one event, and the event is confirmed only if at least 3 of the 5 stations alarmed. This page shows confirmed events only.
